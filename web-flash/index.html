Flash Stock Tracker to your ESP32-S3

This installs the firmware directly from your browser over USB — no Arduino IDE, no terminal.

  1. Plug your ESP32-S3 into this computer via USB.
  2. Click the button below and pick the right serial port when your browser asks.
  3. Wait for it to finish — the device reboots into the new firmware automatically.

Needs Chrome, Edge, or another Chromium-based browser (uses the Web Serial API — Firefox and Safari don't support this yet). If the button above says your browser isn't supported, that's why.

Already have a device running an older version? You don't need this page — just use the Firmware tab in the desktop app to update wirelessly instead. This page is mainly for flashing a brand new board for the first time.
